01 THE PROJECT

Turning job listings
into useful context.

TechPulse collects technology job listings and organises them into a clearer view of the hiring landscape. Instead of looking at each vacancy in isolation, it brings together recurring skills, role categories, companies, locations and available salary information.

The goal is simple: make it easier to explore what employers are asking for, spot patterns in the listings, and ask better questions about the technology job market.

03 HOW TO READ IT

Useful signals.
Honest limits.

TechPulse is based on a targeted collection of online job listings. It is a snapshot of the records gathered by the project, not a complete census of vacancies or a definitive measure of national hiring demand.

Role families and skill mentions are inferred from listing text. Salary fields may be missing, inconsistent or expressed on different bases. Treat the charts as exploratory evidence, and use the methodology section in the dashboard for context.
